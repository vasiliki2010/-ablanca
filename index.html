The 10 minute reset

Do you feel overwhelmed?

Step 1

Stop! Put your phone down and take a pose

Step 2

Breath. Take a slow, deep breath in...and out

Step 3

Move your body. Strech, stand up or walk around

Step 4

Eat. Choose a fruit or your favourite healthy snack

Step 5

Put cold water or an ice pack on your face

Step 6

Write down 3 things you are grateful for

Step 7

Notice 3 things you can see hear or feel

Step 8

Get some fresh air

Step 9

Chew a piece of gum

Step 10

Squeeze a stress ball

What do you want to do next?

Choose intentionally
