The 10 minute reset!

Do you feel overwhelmed?

Step 1 out of 10

Stop! Put your phone down and take a pose

Step 2 out of 10

Take a slow, deep breath in...and out

Step 3 out of 10

Move your body. Stretch, stand up or walk around

Step 4 out of 10

Eat, choose a fruit or your favourite healthy snack

Step 5 out of 10

Put cold water or an ice pack on your face

Step 6 out of 10

Notice 3 things you can see, hear, or feel right now

Step 7 out of 10

Write down 3 things you are grateful for

Step 8 out of 10

Get some fresh air

Step 9 out of 10

Chew a piece of gum.

Step 10 out of 10

Squeeze a stress ball

What do you actually want to do next?

Choose intentionally

  1. Continue what i was doing

  2. Take a longer break

  3. Get ready for bed

  4. Return to my phone intentionally
